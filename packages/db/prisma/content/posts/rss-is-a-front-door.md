Some of our readers have never seen our website. They read every post in a feed reader, in a layout they chose, at a time they chose. For them the feed is the publication.

## The whole post, not a teaser

Our feed carries the full text of each post. A feed of excerpts that forces a click through to the site treats feed readers as a marketing channel. We would rather they read in peace.

## Sanitized like the page

The feed entry is produced by the same markdown pipeline as the page and cleaned by the same sanitizer. A feed reader is a browser of sorts, and an unsafe link or a script in a feed entry is as dangerous there as it is here.

## Dates that do not move

Each entry has a stable id and the date the post was first published. Correcting a typo does not re-date the post or push it back to the top of anyone's unread list.
