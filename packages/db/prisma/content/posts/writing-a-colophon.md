At the back of a well-made book there is often a short note saying how the book was made: the typeface, the paper, the printer, sometimes the press. That note is the colophon. We think a website can keep the habit.

## What ours says

Our colophon page lists the typefaces we use and their licences, the fact that every page is built ahead of time, the software that renders our markdown, and how to reach the people who run the site. It is short. It changes when we change something.

## Why bother

Partly courtesy. A reader who likes how a page looks should be able to find out why without opening developer tools. Partly accountability. Writing down how the site is made is a quick way to notice when we have started doing something we would rather not admit to, like loading a tracker on every page.

## What not to put in it

A colophon is not an about page and not a changelog. It answers one question, how was this made, and stops.
