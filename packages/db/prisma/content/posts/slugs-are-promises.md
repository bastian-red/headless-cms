A slug is the readable part of a post's address: the `slugs-are-promises` at the end of this one. Once a post is published, people link to it. Those links are not ours to break.

## Changing one is allowed

Titles get better after publication. When an editor renames a post and its slug, the old slug is kept as a redirect to the new one. Every old link still works and lands on the new address.

## Reusing one is not

An old slug stays reserved for the post it belonged to. If a new post could take it, a three-year-old link would quietly land on a different article. Our database refuses that: a slug cannot be both a live post and a redirect.

## Good slugs

Short, lowercase, words separated by hyphens, and free of dates unless the date is the point. The studio suggests one from the title, folding accented letters to their plain forms, and the editor can change it before publishing.
