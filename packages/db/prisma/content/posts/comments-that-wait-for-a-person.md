Every comment on this site is read by an editor before anyone else can see it. You do not need an account to comment. You do need to wait.

## Why moderate everything

Unmoderated comment sections fill with spam within days and with worse within weeks. Moderating after the fact means the worst comments are public until someone notices. Moderating first means a reader never sees what an editor would have removed.

## What the wait looks like

When you submit a comment, the page tells you it is waiting for review. It does not appear on the page, not even to you, until an editor approves it. When it is approved, the post's page is rebuilt with your comment on it.

## What we keep

Your name and your comment. We do not keep your address. For rate limiting we keep a keyed hash of it, which lets us notice a flood from one place without being able to tell where that place is.
