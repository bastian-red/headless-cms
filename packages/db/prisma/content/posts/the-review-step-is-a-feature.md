On this site an author can write, save and submit. An author cannot publish. Only an editor can put a post in front of readers.

## Not a lack of trust

Every author here is trusted. The review step exists because a second reader catches things the writer cannot see: the sentence that only makes sense with context the reader does not have, the claim that needs a source, the headline that promises more than the post delivers.

## What review looks like

An author submits a post and it moves to review. An editor reads it in the same preview the public will see, with the search result and the social card beside it. The editor can send it back with a note, schedule it, or publish it.

## What it costs

A few hours, usually. Sometimes a day. We think that is a fair price for never publishing something we have to take down an hour later.

The permissions live in one table that the server, the studio and the tests all read, so an author cannot publish by finding a button someone forgot to hide.
