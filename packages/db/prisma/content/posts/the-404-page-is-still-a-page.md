Someone followed a link to us and it did not lead anywhere. Maybe the link was mistyped. Maybe we took the post down. Either way, a person is looking at the page we sent.

## Answer honestly

The not-found page answers with status 404. A not-found page that answers 200 tells search engines it is a real page with thin content, and they may index it at the broken address.

## Offer a way on

The page says plainly that nothing is here, and offers the search box and the latest posts. It does not guess at what the reader wanted and redirect them somewhere that might be wrong.

## Renamed is not missing

A post that was renamed is not missing. Its old address redirects to the new one, so a reader with an old link never sees the not-found page at all.

## Accessible like any other page

The not-found page has a heading, a landmark and a skip link, and it passes the same accessibility checks as every other page on the site.
