Our studio will not publish an image without a text alternative. That rule creates a small amount of work, and we would rather explain the work than pretend it is not there.

## What alt text is for

Alt text is what a screen reader says instead of showing the image. It is also what appears when the image fails to load. It should say what the image contributes to the post, not what it contains pixel by pixel.

A chart in a post about load times does not need "a bar chart with blue bars". It needs the point the chart makes: "Load time fell from 2.4 seconds to 0.9 after the change."

## When to leave it empty

A purely decorative image, one that adds nothing a reader would miss, can have empty alt text so screen readers skip it. Our posts rarely have those, so the studio asks for a reason before accepting an empty alternative.

## How long

Usually a sentence. If an image needs a paragraph to explain, the explanation probably belongs in the post itself, where everyone can read it.
