Every post here has an excerpt: one or two sentences written by the author. It appears on the home page, on tag pages, in search results and in social previews.

## Not the first paragraph

The first paragraph of a post is written for someone who has already decided to read. The excerpt is written for someone deciding. It answers one question: what will I get if I read this?

## Short enough to survive

Search results and social cards cut text off at different lengths. The studio counts characters and shows the excerpt as each place will show it, so the author sees the cut before readers do.

## When it is also the description

If the author does not write a separate search description, the excerpt is used. That is usually fine, and it is one less field to fill in.
