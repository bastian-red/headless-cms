A search result is a promise that the page answers the question. The snippet under the title is the evidence.

## Show the match

Our snippets come from the part of the post that matched, with the matching words marked. A reader can tell at a glance whether the post uses the word the way they meant it.

## Do not show markup

The snippet is built from the post's plain text, not its markdown. A snippet that shows asterisks and link syntax is noise.

## Mark safely

The marks around matched words are added after the text is escaped. A post that contains angle brackets is shown as text, never interpreted. A snippet is the easiest place on a site to introduce an injection by accident, so it gets its own test.
